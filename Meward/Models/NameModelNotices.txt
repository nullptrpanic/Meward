ChinesePersonName is a Meward model trained with synthetic chats, instructions, forms, dictionary terms and annotated sentence contexts from the following sources.

MultiCoNER v2
Fetahu, Chen, Kar, Rokhlenko and Malmasi (2023). MultiCoNER v2: a Large Multilingual Dataset for Fine-grained and Noisy Named Entity Recognition.
https://huggingface.co/datasets/MultiCoNER/multiconer_v2
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The Chinese training split was converted to simplified characters and its person subtypes were combined into a single name category.

MASSIVE 1.1
Copyright Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates. The data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://github.com/alexa/massive
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The Simplified Chinese training partition was adapted by substituting generated people into annotated person slots. MASSIVE localizes utterances from SLURP, also licensed under CC BY 4.0.
https://github.com/pswietojanski/slurp
FitzGerald et al., MASSIVE, https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08582
Bastianelli et al., SLURP, https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.588/

jieba dictionary (revision 67fa2e36e72f69d9134b8a1037b83fbb070b9775)
https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba
The training script sampled ordinary words, modifiers, place names and organisation names from the dictionary. Given-name fragments from other entries were recombined with surnames to generate examples. Annotated personal names and their sentence contexts came from MultiCoNER.
