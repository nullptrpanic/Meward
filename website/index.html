Meward support

Add a watermark or cover personal details in a photo on your device.

Add a watermark

Choose a locally stored image with Choose photo, or enter your watermark text and tap the camera. Use Tile for repeated text, or place it in the center or a corner. Clear the text to remove the watermark.

Cover personal details

Open Redact to look for Chinese names, mainland Chinese mobile numbers and resident ID numbers. Check each suggestion. Tap a cover to move or resize it, turn off a suggestion to reveal it, or tap Add cover to draw your own.

Preview, save or share

Preview shows the finished image. Save adds a full-resolution PNG copy to Photos. Share opens the system share sheet with the same file. The watermark sits above any covers.

Contact support

For help, email liyufeiat010@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, what you tried and what happened. You can describe the issue using fictional details.

Meward 使用支持

在自己的设备上给照片加水印，或遮挡个人信息。

添加水印

点击 Choose photo 选择本机已有的照片，或先输入水印文字，再点相机拍照。Tile 会平铺水印，也可以选择居中或四个角的位置。清空文字即可移除水印。

遮挡个人信息

打开 Redact，可在本机识别疑似中文姓名、中国大陆手机号和居民身份证号。请逐项检查。点选遮挡框可以移动或缩放，关闭单项遮挡可以恢复显示。点 Add cover 可手动补画。

预览、保存和分享

Preview 显示最终效果。Save 将原图像素尺寸的 PNG 副本添加到相册，Share 使用同一份文件打开系统分享菜单。水印会显示在所有遮挡层上方。

联系支持

如需帮助，可邮件联系 liyufeiat010@gmail.com，并说明 iOS 版本、操作步骤和遇到的问题。使用虚构资料描述问题即可。
